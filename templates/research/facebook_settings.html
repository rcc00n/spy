What collection covers

Each discovery pass checks up to 10 links per source. Comment refresh targets 40 visible comments per discussion. Continuation reopens known threads with a larger budget. Collection is limited to accessible public content and cannot cover all of Facebook. Video and images are not analyzed.

Pausing stops future scheduled steps; an in-flight step may finish saving. One-time scans are managed separately. Access challenges pause automatic collection until you resume it.
